Al Madina Mandi

Our Story

In 2012, Tariq, a retired bus driver, dug a six-foot pit in a small garage, aiming to introduce slow-roasted Arabian Mandi to a city obsessed with Biryani. For months, business stalled—locals found the subtle spices too mild. To adapt, Tariq made two key moves: Introduced a spiced pan-dripping gravy to create the signature "Juicy Mandi." Served free, piping-hot mutton broth (maraq) to every customer upon seating. The turning point came during a 2014 monsoon storm. A popular local cricket team got stranded outside, ducked into the shop, and devoured three giant platters. The team captain posted a photo of the feast online, calling it the ultimate comfort food. The post went viral. By the next morning, lines stretched around the block. Tariq expanded the tiny garage into a three-story restaurant with private carpeted cabins, turning Al Madina Mandi into a legendary dining institution.
